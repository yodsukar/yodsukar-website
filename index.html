About Rich

I’ve experienced every stage of video production throughout my 15+ years in the industry and that start-to-finish background breathes life into a rough idea.

From pre-production and planning, on-set work as a camera operator, director, and gaffer, and post-production in video editing and motion graphics, through to final delivery and distribution, the detail and precision never stops.

Whether it’s an end-to-end PR campaign, long-form brand and event storytelling, or fast-paced vertical content for paid and organic social, I love telling stories that create results.
